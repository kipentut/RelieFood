Food Storage Tips

🍚 Rice Storage ▼
🫙

Use Airtight Containers

Prevents moisture and insects.

📅

Consume Quickly

Eat refrigerated rice within 1-3 days.

🔥

Reheat Once Only

Avoid repeated reheating.

🌡️

Store in Cool Areas

Keep rice away from sunlight and heat.

🥄

Use Clean Scoops

Avoid contamination from dirty utensils.

📦

Practice FIFO Method

Use older rice before newer stock.

🚫

Avoid Leaving Outside

Do not leave cooked rice at room temperature too long.

🥬 Vegetables ▼
🧻

Wrap Leafy Greens

Use paper towels to absorb moisture.

🌡️

Store Properly

Keep root crops in cool, dark places.

👀

Organize Fridge

Keep vegetables visible to avoid forgetting them.

🍅

Separate Ethylene Producers

Tomatoes and bananas can speed up spoilage.

🧺

Use Ventilated Containers

Allows airflow and keeps vegetables fresh longer.

🚿

Wash Before Use Only

Washing early increases moisture buildup.

📆

Consume Older Produce First

Prevents vegetables from being wasted.

🍖 Meat Storage ▼
🧼

Avoid Cross-Contamination

Separate raw meat from other foods.

🏷️

Label Dates

Track storage and expiration dates.

❄️

Thaw Safely

Use refrigerator or cold water only.

🌡️

Keep in Coldest Area

Store meat at the bottom shelf of the fridge.

📦

Use Sealed Containers

Prevents leaks and odor spread.

Follow Storage Limits

🔥

Cook Thoroughly

Proper cooking helps prevent foodborne illness.

📦 General Tips ▼
🧼

Avoid Cross Contamination

Prevent bacteria from one food (e.g., raw meat) from spreading to others (e.g., vegetables). Risk: food poisoning. Tip: Store raw foods separately from ready-to-eat foods.

🥡

Keep Food Sealed or Wrapped

Store food in airtight containers or wrap properly. Applies to meat, fish, soups, and leftovers. Exception: Vegetables should be removed from bags to “breathe” and avoid spoilage.

❄️

Avoid Refreezing Defrosted Food

Thawing can introduce bacteria and refreezing increases bacterial growth risk. Tip: Cook immediately after thawing and portion food before freezing.

🌡️

Maintain Proper Temperature

Fridge: ≤ 5°C. Freezer: ≤ –15°C. Maintaining these temperatures helps slow bacterial growth.

🫙

Keep Dry Goods Dry

Includes rice, pasta, oatmeal — store in sealed containers to prevent mold, bacteria, and pests (e.g., cockroaches).

🚮

Discard Suspicious Food

If unsure, throw it away to prevent food poisoning from spoiled food.

🧹

Keep Storage Areas Clean

Dirty storage leads to bacteria, mold, and pests. Cleaning frequency: Fridge — every 3–4 months; Cupboards — at least once a year.

🥗 Foods That Spoil Fastest ▼
🍓

Berries

Shelf life: up to ~8 days (refrigerated). Store in fridge and throw away entirely if mold appears — it spreads below the surface.

🍌

Bananas

Shelf life: ~2–5 days (counter). Keep ripe and unripe separate, use brown paper bags to speed ripening, and use overripe bananas for banana bread.

🍅

Tomatoes

Shelf life: ~1 week (counter). Do NOT refrigerate; store at room temperature to avoid soft or mealy texture.

🍑

Peaches

Shelf life: short (few days when ripe). Refrigerate when ripe and keep away from ethylene-producing fruits.

🥔

Potatoes

Shelf life: longer if stored properly. Do NOT refrigerate; store in a cool, dry cupboard.

🥑

Avocados

Shelf life: 3–4 days (counter), 7–10 days (fridge). Refrigerate once ripe and check for black/brown color or dents.

🫘

Green Beans

Shelf life: ~5–7 days (fridge). Keep dry, store in an open bag with paper towel, and keep away from ethylene-producing produce.

🥬

Kale

Shelf life: short (wilts quickly). Store in a plastic bag with paper towel and keep in the produce drawer.

🥤

Cold-Pressed Juices

Shelf life: ~few days. Always refrigerate and drink quickly because nutrients degrade fast.

🥦

Broccoli

Shelf life: ~7–10 days (fridge). Needs air circulation, so don’t seal tightly, and keep away from fruits.

🍄

Mushrooms

Shelf life: whole up to ~10 days, sliced ~5 days. Store in a paper bag, not plastic, and keep dry.

🌿

Asparagus

Shelf life: ~2 days. Trim bottoms and store upright in water with the tops covered.

🥣

Hummus

Shelf life: ~3–4 days. Store airtight and prevent contamination due to Listeria risk.

🍚

Cooked Grains

Shelf life: ~4–6 days. Store in airtight containers and discard if left out more than 2 hours.

🥚

Hard-Boiled Eggs

Shelf life: ~1 week (fridge). Discard if left out more than 2 hours.

🥦

Cooked Vegetables

Shelf life: ~3–7 days. Store airtight; high-water vegetables spoil faster.

🥓

Deli Meats

Shelf life: ~3–5 days. Store in coldest part of fridge and handle carefully due to Listeria risk.

🌿

Parsley

Shelf life: short (very delicate). Trim stems, store in water like a plant, and cover loosely to retain moisture.

🥛

Yogurt

Shelf life: short (spoils easily). Keep refrigerated and consume soon after purchase.

🐟

Fish

Shelf life: ~1–2 days. Store on ice in the fridge, replace melted ice, and consume quickly.
