Food Storage & Management Tips

Learn how to properly store ingredients to maximize freshness and minimize waste.

🍚 Rice Storage ▼
Store rice in airtight containers and keep it in a cool, dry place.
🥬 Vegetable Storage ▼
Refrigerate leafy vegetables and keep them dry to avoid spoilage.
🍖 Meat Storage ▼
Store meat in the freezer if not used immediately. Keep it sealed properly.
📦 General Food Waste Prevention Tips ▼
Plan meals, check expiration dates, and store food correctly.
