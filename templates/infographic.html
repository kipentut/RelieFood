Common Wasted Foods in the Philippines

Studies analyzing household plate waste in the Philippines have identified specific food items that are most frequently wasted:

🍚

Rice

Accounts for a significant portion of household food waste due to over-preparation.

🥬

Vegetables

Often spoil before use due to forgotten storage or lack of meal planning.

🍖

Meat

Partially used meat products remain unused after meal preparation.

Source: Angeles-Agdeppa et al., 2023
